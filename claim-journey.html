From loss to supported response

One record that becomes more complete as the claim develops.

Document the property

Bring in photographs, receipts, reports, policy documents and correspondence. Keep the originals connected to the claim.

Build the working scope

Select repair concerns, add measurements and mark anything you still need to confirm as missing, incomplete or uncertain.

Develop the estimate

Use itemized labor, materials and equipment with regional pricing, supplier quotes and inflation context identified in the report.

Add the carrier estimate

Keep the carrier version separate. Compare scope, quantities, line prices, recoverable depreciation, deductible and payments received.

Prepare the response

Show the signed contractor-versus-carrier RCV gap, explain the supported differences and create a demand-letter draft from the organized record.

Keep the record ready

Continue adding correspondence and evidence. If professional or legal review becomes necessary, the claim does not have to be rebuilt from the beginning.
